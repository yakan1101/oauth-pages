Family Drive Automation

This is a private, non-commercial tool used only by one family to automate the organization of files in the family's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the family uses it.

What it does

  • Saves and organizes documents in the family's own Google Drive.
  • Runs only on the family's own computer.

概要（日本語）

この接続は、1つの家族が自分たちのGoogleドライブ内のファイル整理を自動化するためだけに使う、非営利の個人用ツールです。一般には提供していません。
